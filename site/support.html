Support

How can we help?

Pricemori is a small, independent app. The fastest way to get help, report a bug, or suggest an improvement is GitHub Issues.

Open GitHub Issues
Reporting a problem? Include your iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and whether the journal was imported or entered manually. Do not attach a backup containing prices you consider private.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pricemori need an account or internet connection?

No. Recording, history, charts, stores, archives, and settings work offline. The App Store is contacted only when you open the donation screen or export through a system service.

Why does a product show no price change?

A change needs two comparable observations at the same branch, currency, unit dimension, and condition. A single observation is a reference price, not a trend.

Why are two prices not compared?

Pricemori does not compare different currencies, weight and volume, items and kilograms, or regular and conditional prices without an explicit basis. Unknown package sizes are kept as package prices and excluded from unit rankings.

How do I back up or move my journal?

Open Journal → Menu → Settings → Backup & export. A full backup is a JSON file that can be restored on another install. CSV is for spreadsheets and does not restore a complete journal.

How does restore work?

Choose a full backup, validate it, review the merge, and resolve any conflicts. Merge is the default. Replacing the journal requires a safety backup first.

Where is my journal stored?

On your iPhone, in the app’s local SwiftData store. The developer has no copy and cannot recover a deleted journal. Use Backup & export to keep a copy you control.

Can I restore a donation?

Donations are optional consumable tips. They do not unlock features and are not restorable. Apple handles the payment and receipt. Contact Apple Support for billing or refund questions.

Why do tip prices differ by country?

The App Store sets local prices and taxes for your storefront. Lower-price storefronts are configured separately so the tip screen reflects local purchasing power.

Are my prices sent anywhere?

No. Pricemori has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or server. See the privacy policy.
