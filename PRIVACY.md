# Pricemori Privacy Policy

**Effective: 8 November 2026**

Pricemori is a personal price journal. It is designed to work without an account, a server, analytics, advertising, or a shared price database.

## What the app stores

Pricemori stores the information you enter on your device:

- products and identifying details,
- store names and branches,
- prices, dates, package sizes, conditions, sources, and notes,
- favorites, archives, and app preferences.

This information is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party by Pricemori.

## What the app does not collect

Pricemori does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There are:

- no user accounts,
- no analytics or telemetry,
- no advertising or tracking,
- no third-party SDKs that collect data,
- no cloud synchronization,
- no external product catalog or price scraping.

The app includes a privacy manifest declaring that it does not track users and does not collect data types.

## Exports and backups

Backups and CSV files are created only when you choose to export them. They are saved to a location you select using the system Files interface. The developer cannot access those files.

## Donations

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment details, receipts, and purchase records under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not receive your name, email address, or payment card details. Apple may provide the developer with aggregate sales and tax information.

Pricemori asks the App Store for localized product prices so the donation screen can display the price for your storefront. This request is handled by StoreKit. Donations are optional, consumable tips and do not unlock features.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling Pricemori removes its local database from your device. Exported backup and CSV files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. The developer has no copy to delete.

## Children

Pricemori is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
